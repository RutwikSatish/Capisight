Proposal: Warehouse Racking Expansion

Adding a third level of selective racking in Bay C will increase pallet positions by about 40 percent. The vendor quote is USD 1.1M all-in. Finance expects the investment to pay for itself in three and a half years, using a discount rate of 9% and a planned life of 15 years. The NPV came out at $430,000. No ROI figure was calculated for this proposal. An earlier alternative (leasing off-site space) was rejected; its five-year cost would have been $1.6M.
